title: Vintage Guitars and Good Music
description: Jan Esterlis shares a passion for vintage guitars, amps, effects, and good music of every genre.
---

On this site, I wish to provide an opportunity for all guitar enthusiasts to share their passion for guitars, as well as music in general, regardless of the genre. The purpose of this website is to serve as an arena for sharing views on collecting vintage guitars, amplifiers and effects.

I hope you will find this site fun, as well as informative. I plan to publish guitar samples sent to me by visitors, and pictures featuring vintage guitars and gear are welcome.

One more thing: I would like to start a meeting place for native Russian-born guitar collectors and musicians. Often Americans don't think that such a category of guitar enthusiasts exists at all, but we can prove them wrong! (Joking…)

So, welcome, my dear guitar friends!
