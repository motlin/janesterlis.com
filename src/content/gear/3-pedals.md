I am a big fan of vintage effects and 70s classic rock sound. This is reflected in the content of my pedal board, which currently changes, but the basic stays the same:

- 70s CryBaby
- Ibanez TS 808 Original
- Ibanez TS9 Original
- Rat overdrive
- TC electronics chorus/flanger
- MXR P90 phaser, also from 70s.
- Boss DD5 digital delay
